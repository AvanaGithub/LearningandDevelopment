This system is the audit evidence trail for ISO 13485 training records under SOP series AMD/QSP04. Every create, edit and correction is logged with user, timestamp and reason.

Module 01 · Employee list

Master record for every employee. IDs come from Zoho; data arrives by HR bulk upload. Click a row to open the full record with training history.

CodeNameEntityDivisionDepartmentDesignationManagerStatusHours FY
Exited employees stay visible to the administrator only and are purged after 2 years. Records are deactivated, never deleted — the audit trail stays intact.

Module 02 · Trainings

Plan the training, then click its row to open it — add participants, generate the attendance QR, and manage the session from there.

CodeTitleCategoryModeTrainerDatesHoursParticipantsStatus

Teal — internal · amber — external. Postponed trainings keep their original planned date visible for plan-vs-actual reporting.

Each training carries a material library (agenda, decks, trainer profile, signed attendance scan) available to participants before and after the session. Mandatory trainings carry a validity period that drives re-training alerts.

Module 04 · Attendance

Attendance runs separately per training, per participant, per day. Participants can self-mark by scanning the training's QR — the scan tags them to the right training automatically — and the organizer or trainer can mark or correct anything, with every change logged.

– not marked · P present · A absent · H half-day. Click a cell to cycle, or use Edit on a row to correct a participant with a recorded reason. Eligibility assumes minimum 75% attendance (open checklist item F3).

Module 05 · Feedback

Each training carries its own feedback form — built here or uploaded. Share it by QR or link; responses tag to that training's form automatically. When completion triggers the auto-e-mail, anyone who already submitted via the QR or link is excluded.

Module 09 · Reports

Standard filters on every report — date range, entity, division, department, location, training type, trainer, employee — with Excel and PDF export. Reports marked live actually generate from this prototype's sample data.

Open decisions (Section K): which reports are e-mailed on a schedule and to whom, and the exact formulas for coverage % and compliance % you'll be held to in audits.

Users & Access

System users sign in with their Zoho credentials (Zoho single sign-on) — no separate passwords to manage, and access ends automatically when the Zoho account is disabled. Access is role-based per the matrix below.

User nameZoho e-mail IDUser typeAccess typeStatus
Access levels — Full access: view, add, edit and deactivate anything (Super admin only). View, Add, Edit: work with records but never remove them. View: read-only, including their team's reports. Every access change is logged with user and timestamp.
